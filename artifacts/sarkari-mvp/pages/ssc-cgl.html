Admit Card OUT

SSC started releasing Tier-1 admit cards from 27 September 2026 onwards. Cards are released region-wise / date-wise, usually 2–3 days before the candidate’s exam date.

How to download

  1. Go to ssc.gov.in
  2. Click Login / Register
  3. Enter Registration Number and Password
  4. Select Combined Graduate Level Examination 2026 → Tier-1 Admit Card / Hall Ticket
  5. Download and take a clear printout

Carry the printed admit card + original photo ID + recent photographs as per instructions.

Answer Key PENDING

Tier-1 answer key is not released yet. After the exam window closes (30 Oct), SSC typically releases the provisional answer key with response sheet, opens an objection window, then publishes the final key.

Expected timeline: November 2026.

Result PENDING

Tier-1 result will be declared after evaluation of the final answer key. Qualified candidates will be eligible for Tier-2.

Keep checking the official SSC website and this page for updates.
